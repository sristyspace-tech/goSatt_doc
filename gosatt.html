What it does

Four tools in one app

Search the whole sky

16,000+ active satellites, updated automatically from a live catalogue. Search by name or NORAD ID.

Live sky tracking

Point your phone up — compass and motion sensors guide you to the satellite's exact position, with the full pass arc drawn on screen.

Pass predictions

Favourite a satellite and see exactly when it next rises over your location, with a live countdown and peak elevation.

Antenna builder

Enter a frequency, get complete build plans for a V-dipole or QFH — cut lengths, angles, and step-by-step instructions, plus the full receive-chain wiring for SDR reception.

Installation

How to install

  1. Tap the "Download APK" button above.
  2. When the download finishes, open the file from your notifications or Files app.
  3. If asked to allow "Install unknown apps", allow it for your browser and come back.
  4. Tap Install — GoSatt is ready in seconds. It will ask for location permission; your location never leaves your phone.
Why isn't it on the Play Store yet? It's on the way — Google requires new apps to complete a closed testing period first. Want to help us get there faster? Join the beta and you'll be one of the first Play Store testers.
Privacy

Your location stays on your phone

GoSatt uses your location and motion sensors only to calculate what's overhead — entirely on your device. Nothing about you is ever transmitted. The only network request the app makes is downloading the public satellite catalogue, which is the same file for every user. Read the full privacy policy.
